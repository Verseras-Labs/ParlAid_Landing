It's really just 3 steps

No spreadsheets, no setup.

Step 1

Pick your games

Browse today's odds across every sport. Tap the games you like — spreads, moneylines, totals, even player props. Live lines from 10 sportsbooks in the US and 10 in Ontario.

Step 2

Decide how many you need right

Pick 3 games but only confident about 2? Set it to “2 out of 3.” ParlAid™ shows the tickets you need, what they cost, and what they pay.

Step 3

Place each ticket at its own sportsbook

ParlAid™ builds your tickets and finds the best price for each one across every sportsbook. One tap opens the book with your picks already in the slip.

Teams, players and prices shown are examples.
